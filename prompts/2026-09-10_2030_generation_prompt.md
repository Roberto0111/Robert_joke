### Page 2 — concrete proof

### Page 3 — cat breaks the story

Same desk, closer low angle. The tuxedo cat plants one white paw directly on Roberto's red pen before it touches the finished folder and looks straight at him. Roberto freezes mid-reach, defensive eyes turned toward the cat. The approved blank folder remains closed and visibly intact. Keep the upper third empty and warm-white; no speech balloon because the locally typeset headline carries the cat's voice.

### Page 4 — vulnerable truth

Same studio late at night with more negative space and muted light. Roberto sits on the pale-wood floor beside an open shelf of orderly blank process binders, knees drawn slightly up, holding one worn old notebook close to his chest. His expression is small, embarrassed, and tired rather than melodramatic. The tuxedo cat sits quietly near his shoe, posture attentive but unsentimental. The red pen is set down far away on the desk. This page should feel still and exposed.

### Page 5 — strongest turn

Same studio in restrained early-morning light. The finished folder stays closed on the desk; Roberto has deliberately placed both hands in his lap instead of reaching for it. He looks at the cat with uncomfortable clarity, not victory. The tuxedo cat sits squarely in the small gap between Roberto and the folder, one white paw resting beside—not on—the folder, calmly auditing the choice. Strongest, simplest composition with the largest clean upper text area. The image must make “not intervening” visible without explaining it.
