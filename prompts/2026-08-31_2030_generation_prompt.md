## Reference structure study (abstract mechanics only)

1. Recognition hook: begins with an uneasy mismatch between sustained effort and uncertainty about what one actually wants.
2. Concrete scene: translates abstract life questions into recognizable choices and repeated daily behavior.
3. Escalation: moves from success and productivity into the personal cost of continuing by default.
4. Emotional turn: redirects attention from public markers of progress to the private reason a person keeps choosing them.
5. Final compression: ends with a compact question that can be reused when facing a consequential choice.

Save/share mechanism: the reference organizes a broad uncertainty into a portable self-question. This run borrows only that five-beat movement. It rejects the reference's broad claims about happiness, wealth, and success as substance for this story.

Originality check: this story concerns a professional certificate already earned, avoidance of its associated work, and buying the next exam course to protect the meaning of past effort. It uses different wording, examples, conclusion, setting, composition, typography, characters, and visual identity from the reference. It also differs from the latest posts about resignation, promotion, handover, job offers, and project launch.
