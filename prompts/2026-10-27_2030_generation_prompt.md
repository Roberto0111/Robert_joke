- **assigned_topic_and_hook_arm:** Friendship: invitations, refusal and small unspoken discomforts. Experiment `organic_v3_opening_20261020_B`, package `Roberto先自曝一句`. - **audience_dilemma:** I want a friend to have fun and feel capable, yet want them to keep knowing I am the stronger player. Helpful advice exposes the condition hidden in my generosity.
- **visible_first_frame_action:** Roberto deliberately removes a black stone from play while smiling politely; his other hand clenches below the game table. His friend has just placed a white stone. The cat notices the mismatch. The complete opening, `Roberto：讓棋還生氣。`, is 14 Unicode characters including Latin speaker and punctuation, below the maximum of 16.

- **specific_sharing_hypothesis:** A viewer may think of the friend who says a game is casual, lets others win, then cannot resist explaining why they could have won. This is a hypothetical recipient, not a request to share and not measured evidence.
- **difference_from_latest20:** No hidden invitation, pet-video intermediary, rushed dinner attendance, reciprocal-effort count, birthday memory test, caller name mistake, apology requiring comfort, family control or consumer guilt. The friend is already present and the shared activity is happening. The tension is manufactured by Roberto's own deliberate concession. Cat is an observer of agency and status, not a substitute friend, driver, household beneficiary or moral judge.
