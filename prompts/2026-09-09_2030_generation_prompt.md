4. Emotional turn: moves from a playful observation into unease about what the observation means.
5. Final compression: condenses the whole sequence into one memorable emotional conclusion.

## Five exact story beats

1. 「年終領了，反而不敢走」
2. 「主管一問，我又接了整年專案」
3. 「貓：獎金是謝禮，還是續約金？」
4. 「我怕領完就走，像只為了錢」
5. 「貓：若沒人評論，你還會接嗎？」

Page 1 is 11 Chinese characters excluding punctuation and presents a concrete contradiction. Page 2 proves it with a visible assignment. Page 3 breaks the unexamined idea that past-work compensation purchases future labor. Page 4 names the fear of moral judgment. Page 5 provides a reusable decision question and makes the earlier acceptance look like reputation management rather than consent.
