## Reference structure study (private production notes)

- Opening tension: uses an irreversible adult-life fact to create immediate attention.
- Escalation: removes familiar external anchors one layer at a time.
- Emotional turn: shifts from ownership and relationships toward personal presence.
- Final compression: reduces the broad argument to one present-tense unit the reader can hold.
- Save/share mechanism: a sequence of short claims culminates in one compact perspective suited to rereading.
- Originality check: the selected story rejects the reference post's mortality, inheritance, possession, lineage, permanence, and carpe-diem substance. It uses a different dilemma (solo dining and perceived judgment), dialogue structure, conclusion, restaurant setting, realistic-comic visual identity, characters, compositions, and wording. No sentence is translated or paraphrased.

Input images: main_character_reference.jpg is the mandatory identity and wardrobe reference; reference carousel images are structural study only and must not be visually imitated.
Primary request: an original polished Taiwanese editorial webcomic showing a continuous quiet evening outside and inside a small warm Taiwanese restaurant. Roberto speaks with a black-and-white tuxedo cat. Exactly one large Traditional Chinese dialogue line per panel.

Continuity: identical faces, wardrobe, restaurant palette, line weight, evening lighting, and character proportions across both pages.
Style/medium: cinematic subdued colorful realistic editorial comic; natural anatomy and acting; soft practical amber lighting with muted teal evening shadows; clean fine ink lines and gently painted texture.
Typography: large bold clean Traditional Chinese sans-serif, black or near-black, centered within generous pale text bands; exact verbatim text, no extra labels, no speech bubbles, no watermark.
Composition: each page has exactly two stacked panels separated by one clean dark horizontal divider. Text stays in the upper safe area of each panel and never overlaps a face. Calm medium-wide and medium framing, varied but continuous poses.
Avoid: generic anime man, Joker character or makeup, chibi style, copied reference layouts, exaggerated reactions, meme typography, explosion lines, stickers, tiny text, Simplified Chinese, extra dialogue, extra panels, logos, Instagram UI.

Page 1 visual: panel 1 outside the restaurant window at dusk, Roberto pauses near the entrance and looks through the warm glass toward a small two-seat table; tuxedo cat sits beside him. Panel 2 closer at the doorway, cat looks up calmly while Roberto keeps one hand near the door handle.

Page 2 visual: panel 3 inside at the two-seat table, Roberto sits with a menu and glances at the empty chair opposite; cat sits naturally on the window ledge or adjacent stool.
