# Roberto Joke: 1,000-Follower Organic Growth Strategy

Start: 2026-08-17

## Baseline

- Target: 1,000 followers
- Current automated baseline: 3 followers
- Recent 15-Reel average: about 84 views and 73 accounts reached
- Recent 15-Reel shares: 1; saves: 0
- Phase 1 is organic validation. Paid promotion remains off until a Reel proves it can earn sharing or saving without ads.

## Publishing Schedule

- One primary Reel per day at 20:30 Asia/Taipei
- Analyze recent performance and update the next controlled experiment before every run
- Publish a 14-18 second vertical Reel; the experiment controls the exact reading time
- Keep a visible first frame with Roberto, the tuxedo cat, and the first complete line; never open on black
- Publish the same Reel to Story after the primary post succeeds

## Content Identity

- Roberto is always the recognizable protagonist.
- The tuxedo cat asks the clarifying question and delivers the concise final perspective.
- Keep the serious life-dialogue series, but allow restrained dry humor when the experiment requests it.
- Use grounded situations people would save or send to one specific friend.
- Use original art and 3-5 relevant hashtags.
- Never copy or closely paraphrase another creator's wording, topic, conclusion, examples, visuals, layout, typography, or branding.

## Controlled Experiments

- Rotate six packages across hooks, topic pillars, conclusion mechanisms, caption CTA, and 14/16/18-second pacing.
- Change only the selected package for that day; keep the characters and four-beat story stable.
- Require at least two samples per package before naming a winner.
- Review weekly. Keep packages that create follower growth, reach, shares, and saves; remove packages that only add posting volume.

## Paid Promotion Gate

- Stay organic until one Reel reaches at least 500 accounts organically, earns at least three combined shares/saves, and reaches a 1% share+save rate.
- When qualified, test no more than two proven Reels in Meta Ads Manager with a small fixed budget.
- Scale only when paid delivery produces profile visits and follows at an acceptable cost; views alone are not success.

## Guardrails

- Do not buy followers, mass-follow, or automate spam comments and DMs.
- Do not claim a 1,000-follower guarantee or manufacture fake engagement.
- Do not increase posting volume to hide weak content.
